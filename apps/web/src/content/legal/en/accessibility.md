## Our target

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Color contrast is checked automatically for every design token (51 checks in CI).
- Status is never shown by color alone: every status has an icon and a text label.
- Every chart has a "View as table" alternative.
- All functions work with a keyboard, with a skip link and visible focus.
- Touch targets are at least 44 by 44 pixels on tablets.

## Known issues

- Spanish is not available yet (planned).
- Some chart tooltips need a mouse; the table view has the same values.
- A full screen reader audit has not been done yet (planned before release).

## Report a barrier

Open an issue at [https://github.com/jayp0671/sterileguard-ai/issues](https://github.com/jayp0671/sterileguard-ai/issues) describing the page and what got in the way.
We aim to respond within 5 business days.
