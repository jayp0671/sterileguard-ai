## What is used

The app uses the department's **total** overtime hours per day for the morning operation, and
daily staffing counts by start time. It does not use time-clock records of individual
employees, and it stores no employee names or ID numbers.

## What it is never used for

- Evaluating, ranking, scheduling or disciplining individual employees
- Monitoring who worked overtime
- Pay or payroll decisions

The Terms of Use forbid these uses.

## Who sees it

Members of your department, according to their role. Supervisors log and approve the daily
totals. Every change is recorded in the audit log.
