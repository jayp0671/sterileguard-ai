## Academic prototype

SterileGuard AI is a student project for a university course. It is provided for teaching,
demonstration and evaluation only.

## Decision support only

The app shows estimates of next-day overtime and possible staffing changes. Every number is a
**model estimate, not a guarantee**. It never makes staffing decisions; people do. Do not rely
on it for clinical, safety, employment or payroll decisions.

## Acceptable use

- Do not enter patient information, or employee names or ID numbers.
- Do not use the app to evaluate, rank or discipline individual employees.
- Do not try to access another department's data, break security controls or overload the
  service.
- Keep your password and authenticator private.

## Accounts

suspend accounts that break these terms.
