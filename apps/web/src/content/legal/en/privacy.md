## Who we are

SterileGuard AI is an academic prototype built by students for CPE 595 at Stevens Institute of
Technology. It forecasts next-day overtime for a hospital Dental Central Sterile department.
It is not a commercial product.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address, an optional display name, your role in a
  department, and your two-factor authentication enrollment (managed by Supabase Auth).
- **Operational counts:** daily totals such as scheduled patients, staff on shift, equipment in
  service and total overtime hours for the department. These are counts, never patient or
  employee records.
- **Logs:** sign-in events, an append-only audit log of changes and exports, and technical
  request logs (request IDs, timings). Logs identify users by an internal ID, not by name.
- **Cookie choices:** whether you accepted optional analytics, with the cookie policy version.

We do **not** collect patient information, employee names or employee IDs, and the product
blocks text that looks like names, contact details or ID numbers.

## Why we use it

To sign you in and keep your account secure, to produce forecasts and staffing scenarios for
your department, to keep an audit trail, and to fix problems. Optional analytics (off by
default) would help us understand which screens are used.

## How long we keep it

See the [Data Retention Policy](/trust/data-retention). Audit logs are kept 6 years; uploaded
import files are deleted after they are committed, or within 24 hours.

## Who processes it

Our hosting and service providers are listed on the [Subprocessors](/trust/subprocessors) page.
We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

## Your rights

Modern US state privacy laws, such as New Jersey's, give you the right to know what we hold
about you, to correct it, to delete it, to get a copy, and to opt out of targeted advertising
and sale (we do neither). Ask your department admin, or open an issue at
[https://github.com/jayp0671/sterileguard-ai/issues](https://github.com/jayp0671/sterileguard-ai/issues) without including personal details, and we will follow up. We
answer within 45 days.

## Contact

Open an issue at [https://github.com/jayp0671/sterileguard-ai/issues](https://github.com/jayp0671/sterileguard-ai/issues). For security matters see the
[Security page](/trust/security).
